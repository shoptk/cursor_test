# Light SEO & Launch Tips for Briefbox

These are organic growth tips — not black-hat tactics.

## On-site basics (confirm on your live site)

- Prefer the canonical https://www.ddm99.xyz/en/ everywhere you promote.
- Keep title/description aligned with: “Temporary Email for Instant Verification”.
- Ensure `robots` allows indexing on marketing pages (root currently redirects; `/en/` should stay `index, follow`).
- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console / Bing Webmaster Tools for `ddm99.xyz` and `www.ddm99.xyz`.
- Make sure HTTPS, redirects (apex ↔ www), and OG tags remain consistent.

## Content that earns links

Write one solid English article you control (blog, Dev.to, or Notion public page), then link out from directories to it or to the product:

- “Why I built a 10-minute disposable email”
- “How temporary email protects your primary inbox”
- “Temp mail vs. aliases (+ when Briefbox is enough)”

Honest comparisons and clear limitations convert better than hype.

## Backlink quality bar

Good: editorial mentions, tool roundups, AlternativeTo, Show HN discussion, your own GitHub README homepage field.  
Bad: PBNs, paid comment spam, unrelated guest posts with exact-match anchors.

Anchor text mix: “Briefbox”, “temporary email”, “disposable inbox”, bare URL — don’t force the same keyword every time.

## Launch week rhythm

1. Day 0: Product Hunt / Show HN / Indie Hackers (pick 1–2, not all at once if you can’t reply).
2. Day 1–2: reply to every comment; fix obvious UX bugs fast.
3. Day 3–7: submit 5–10 high-quality directories from `directories.md`.
4. Ongoing: one useful post or update per week beats daily spam.

## Metrics to watch

- Referral traffic by listing URL (Umami is already on the site)
- Signup/verification success rate (does mail arrive within the 10-minute window?)
- Bounce on landing — if high, tighten headline to match ads/posts

## Compliance reminder

Position Briefbox for **privacy and legitimate temporary verification**. Do not market it as a way to evade bans, abuse trials, or break other products’ terms.
