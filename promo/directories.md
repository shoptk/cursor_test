## Launch & maker communities

| Place | Why | Notes |
|-------|-----|--------|
| [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) | Launch exposure | Need a maker account; prepare tagline + first comment from `social.md` |
| [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/) | Maker feedback | Post in Product launches / Ask IH |
| [Hacker News – Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/submit) | Technical audience | Follow Show HN etiquette; engage in comments |
| [Dev.to](https://dev.to/) | Long-form launch post | Write “I built a temp-mail tool…” with honest tradeoffs |

| [BetaList](https://betalist.com/) | Startup discovery | Startup / tool listing |

## Do not do

- Mass-posting the same link across unrelated forums
- Buying “1000 dofollow backlinks” packages
- Comment spam on blogs (“Great post! Check my temp mail…”)
- Fake accounts or sockpuppet upvotes
- Impersonating other brands or claiming partnerships you don’t have
